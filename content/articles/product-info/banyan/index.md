Banyan is a website template and Hugo theme for documentation, articles, products and resource directories. It organizes content through sections and article references, and publishes static pages without a database.

## Organize content

- **Nested categories**: build a section hierarchy and browse related content through breadcrumb columns.
- **Multiple categories**: include an article in several sections while maintaining one copy of its content.
- **Flexible presentation**: choose article lists, product lists, aggregate lists or image walls.

## Customize the site

Banyan supports multiple languages and light and dark appearances. Customize colors, icons, navigation and article information, or build a homepage with a home art bundle.

Pagefind provides full-text search. RSS, share images, sitemaps and llms.txt help readers discover and follow content. Offline access and page prefetching are optional.

## Build and publish

Hugo generates static pages. The companion swaw-banyan Skill provides preview, search indexing and checks on Windows x64, Linux x64 and macOS x64/ARM64. A local AI agent can help maintain the site.

Publish to Cloudflare Pages, EdgeOne Makers or another static host. Banyan generates delivery configuration for the first two platforms and supports CSP and SRI.

## Scope

Use Banyan for knowledge bases, project documentation, personal writing, product information and resource directories. Product lists display information and prices. Accounts, payments and comments require separate backend services.

Banyan is free to use and modify under the MIT license. The first official release is in preparation.
