swban server/lan [Hugo arguments...]  #Start development for LAN access

Same as dev, but listens on IPv6 wildcard ::, also accepting IPv4 where supported.
Connect the phone to the same LAN and use the computer's LAN address and port.

swban server/lan --bind 0.0.0.0       #Listen on IPv4 only
swban server/lan -p 8080              #Use port 8080
