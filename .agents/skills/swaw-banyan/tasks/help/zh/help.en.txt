swban help/zh [depth|path [depth]]  #Show Chinese help

With no argument, show common commands.
Paths ending in / are categories; other paths are executable commands.
depth  Maximum depth below path, or below the root when path is omitted
path   Command, category or topic for full help

swban help/zh 2                #List paths up to two levels
swban help/zh check 2          #List two levels below check
swban help/zh check/browser 3  #List three levels below check/browser
swban help/zh check/csp        #Show full command help
